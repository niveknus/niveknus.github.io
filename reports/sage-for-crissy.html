What is this? ✨

Sage lives inside the ChatGPT app you already use. Same app, one tab over. You type, it answers, exactly like you're used to.

The difference: this one has your whole PhD in its memory. Your classes, your notes, your homework, your Zoom links, the school's rules, all of it. It never forgets and it never loses anything.

💡 The simplest way to think about it: you talk, Sage does the paperwork.

How you talk to it 💬

Just like texting a very organized friend. No special words, no buttons to learn.

You
hey! what's my week look like?
Sage
Morning! 🌿 Saturday is Treatment of Sexual Dysfunctions, part 1, with Dr. Clark. Zoom link is already in your calendar. Quick heads up: there's homework to do BEFORE class this time (an Esther Perel talk, plus 3 takeaways). Want me to pull it up?
You
ugh I can't make Oct 10
Sage
No problem! That same class runs again Sunday Oct 18 with Dr. Clark. Want me to switch you? I'll move your calendar and keep the reminder.

yes please 🙏 also remind me what trauma bonding vs stockholm was?
Sage
Done, you're on for Oct 18 📅 And yes: that came up in your Human Trafficking class. They're related but they form differently... (want the podcast version for the car? 🎧)

What it does for you 🗓️

What the class covers, and how it connects to classes you've already taken. Read it the night before with your tea, or don't. Zero pressure.
You already keep a ChatGPT window open during class to look things up. Keep doing exactly that, just in the Sage tab. 💚 The difference: everything you ask gets saved into your class notes automatically.

If something ever glitches 🔧

Short version: you're not stuck, and you're not waiting on anyone.

🛠️
Sage knows how to fix itself
It has its own instruction manual built in. If something stops working, just say "that didn't work" and it will figure out why and repair it.
🔑
Everything is in your name
Your Google account, your calendar, your files. So if it ever needs permission again, you can give it, right from your own laptop. No one else has to be involved.

Sage will write out a plain English note about what went wrong that you can send to Kevin. But that's the backup plan, not the first step.

What Sage will never do 🤍

🎓

Your degree stays 100% yours

Your school's rule is clear: no AI on exams or homework. So Sage will quiz you, explain things, dig up your notes, and cheer you on. But it will never write an answer you turn in. Not one sentence. That's your work, and it should be.
